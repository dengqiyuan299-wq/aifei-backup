# 邓家

## Purpose
The user's household: husband and wife run the rented three-story 初羽茶盧 building in Shijiazhuang — tea rooms on the first and second floors, a salon space on the third — and raise two school-age children there. The family is the centre of the user's daily life, and the couple's real story is the material for the brand's Douyin series.

## Members
- 皇上: the user; owns the 初羽茶盧 brand and runs the building with his wife.
- [茶室女主人](../people/cha-shi-nv-zhu-ren.md): the user's wife; runs the second-floor tea room 凤鸣.
- [邓筱斐](../people/deng-xiao-fei.md): second-grade daughter; learns on the iPad with a dedicated 点读学习台.
- [邓梓浩](../people/deng-zi-hao.md): seventh-grade son; learns through the 梓浩学堂 app.

## Dynamics
- Husband and wife run the building as a partnership: he takes the first floor, she the second-floor tea room 凤鸣; the building is rented since May 2026.
- The father is closely involved in both children's learning — each child has a dedicated learning chat and a standalone learning platform built for them, and he teaches the daughter's class himself for the parent-class lesson.
- The couple's real story is the material for the Douyin series 《一楼烟火，二楼茶》, in which they play the married leads 凤姐 and 元哥.

## Themes
- The three-story building and the 初羽茶盧 tea business.
- The children's schooling: the daughter's phonics, maths and poetry sets, and the son's history and geography.
- The couple's story as the brand's narrative.
