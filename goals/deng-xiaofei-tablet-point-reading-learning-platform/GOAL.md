# Deng Xiaofei tablet point-reading learning platform

Goal ID: goal_30eabdab69f9
Goal slug: deng-xiaofei-tablet-point-reading-learning-platform

## Description
Tablet point-reading closed-loop learning for Deng Xiaofei's Grade 2 study, covering English phonics, maths, and Chinese poetry and characters. It runs point-reading study, recording and self-test, quizzes, error-book re-practice, and spaced review with progress saved on the device.
