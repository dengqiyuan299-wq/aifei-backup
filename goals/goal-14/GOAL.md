# 九年古诗文阅读复习体系

Goal ID: goal_a2ae9a69b59b
Goal slug: goal-14

## Description
Systematic list and review system for the key classical poetry and prose across nine years of primary and junior-high school, based on the 2022 curriculum standard's 135 required poems. It groups poems by theme and grade, sets a daily reading rhythm and spaced review, and gives weekly plans for both children.
