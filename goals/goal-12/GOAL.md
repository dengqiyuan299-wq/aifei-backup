# 邓梓浩初中历史听学

Goal ID: goal_6fa6e3f05615
Goal slug: goal-12

## Description
A story-based history listening app covering all six junior-high history volumes, with a time-river story and a three-step method for dating, causes, and echoes. The sample includes a prologue and four Grade 7 first-volume episodes, with animation, continuous play, and self-tests in a female voice. The remaining five volumes will follow the same model after the user approves the sample.
