## Description
皇上 wants real-time voice conversation with 爱妃. In-app real-time voice is not enabled on his account, so an early-access application was filed and he then chose the self-hosted route: a voice version of 爱妃 built on the VPS with offline speech recognition, a DeepSeek brain carrying 爱妃's persona, and offline voice synthesis, reachable from a phone web page.
